## IX

This is one of the 14 market halls built in Berlin during the 1800s. While many were destroyed during the war or neglected over time as they fell behind the trends, some have been reconstructed through community-led efforts.

Aside from DM, you won't find any major corporate franchises at Markthalle Neun. Despite that—or perhaps because of it—the place is packed with locals, travelers, and Kreuzberg hipsters every Street Food Thursday and Saturday afternoon.

In this post, I’ll share a few spots I visit often.

## Food Stalls

### DM
This is where I buy my water.

### Frisch Gefischt

![Frisch Gefischt](https://markthalleneun.de/media/pages/markt/frisch-gefischt/05bb5a946f-1723806314/frisch-gefischt-289-2048x-q85.webp)

You can get sushi-grade fish here that's safe to eat raw. They also serve fish and chips, which is good enough to be worth a dedicated trip. Just a heads-up: it occasionally disappears from the menu.

### Big Stuff Smoked BBQ

![Big Stuff Smoked BBQ](https://markthalleneun.de/media/pages/markt/big-stuff-smoked-bbq/aa4010b474-1723646084/big-stuff-8-2048x-q85.webp)

The pulled pork seems to be the popular choice, but I personally prefer the pork belly or beef brisket. You can have it served platter-style on a tray, or as a sandwich—their brioche buns are excellent.

### Sironi

![Sironi](https://markthalleneun.de/media/pages/markt/sironi/9f45e0c47c-1724241517/sironi-25-2048x-q85.webp)

I usually go here for pizza or focaccia. I’m a big fan of the mortadella.

### Zoom Fresh

![Zoom Fresh](https://markthalleneun.de/media/pages/markt/zoom-fresh/8d4327ece8-1723814654/zoom-fresh-7-2048x-q85.webp)

A kimchi shop run by a Korean owner. You can grab a bowl of Janchi-guksu (warm noodle soup) for under 10 EUR.
