## Ready-To-Drink/Eat

Just like electronics, protein powder prices vary a lot depending on where you buy it (online/offline) and whether a promotion applies. As someone who shops without a plan, I usually grab products I can consume right away, unless there 'happens' to be a promotion and I 'happen' to be in the mood to buy. Of course, there are powders at prices I can live with even without a promotion, and ready-to-eat products cost much more per unit. Still, I consider it the price of convenience and my own laziness, and I buy them quite often.

This post introduces the private label products among them, which you can always find at relatively low prices in supermarkets and drugstores.

## Supermarket Private Labels

### Lidl

#### Milbona High Protein Drink

![Milbona High Protein Drink](https://sortiment.lidl.ch/media/catalog/product/cache/a4ee2587a2ccecf55b6fb41febdedaf3/5/0/5007051_Schoko_PSXX_1.jpg){: size="50%"}
<p class="caption">©️ Lidl official website</p>

The tastiest and the easiest to consume. Stores almost always carry chocolate, strawberry, banana, and vanilla, and every now and then, or at Lidl in other countries, you can find some new flavors. For example, a Skyr flavor (not for me) shows up at my local Lidl from time to time, and I once saw a melon kefir flavor (not for me) at a Lidl in Spain.

But my favorite is banana. It's similar to Korean banana milk, just a bit thinner. Strawberry and chocolate are also tasty, maybe because they're packed with 'real' sugar, so you notice the artificial flavoring and sweeteners of protein powder less.

Based on the strawberry flavor, 34.98 g of protein and 17.49 g of sugar per 330 ml (350 g).

### REWE

#### REWE Beste Wahl High Protein Pudding

![REWE Beste Wahl High Protein Pudding](https://img.rewe-static.de/8410940/44466804_digital-image.png){: size="50%"}
<p class="caption">©️ Rewe official website</p>

REWE doesn't have any drinks, only puddings, so I once ordered a whole month's worth with a delivery order. I liked that they were cheap at under 1 euro and came in various flavors like chocolate, vanilla, pistachio, and hazelnut. In the end, though, having to eat them with a spoon turned out to be much more inconvenient than I expected (especially when I wanted to eat one somewhere other than at home). The texture is also very thick and heavy, and the portion is bigger than I thought, so it's not really suitable for slurping down after a workout.

Chocolate is the best, and the other flavors are meh... The chocolate mousse was tasty and had a lighter texture, but it contains less protein at 15 g.

Based on the chocolate flavor, 20.00 g of protein and 8.40 g of sugar per 200 g.

#### REWE Beste Wahl High Protein Quarkcreme
![REWE Beste Wahl High Protein Quarkcreme](https://img.rewe-static.de/9435640/56311798_digital-image.png){: size="50%"}
<p class="caption">©️ Rewe official website</p>

Recently, I went to a huge REWE in East Side Mall and bought one peach dragon fruit and one strawberry, since I had never seen the product before. I was happy, thinking REWE finally had something drinkable (at least I expected something like runny yogurt, as pictured on the packaging), only to find out at home that it was Quarkcreme. In Korea, I only ever had Myprotein powder or Kirkland protein bars, so I'd never come across it. In Germany, however, Quarkcreme seems to be common in everyday life and is often eaten for extra protein. Still, squeezing out a really heavy cream and eating it felt strange to me.

Surprisingly, both taste pretty decent. There's a vanilla flavor too.

Based on the peach dragon fruit flavor, 25 g of protein and 6.2 g of sugar per 200 g.

## Drugstore Private Labels

### Rossmann

#### WellMix Proteinriegel

![WellMix Proteinriegel](https://www.rossmann.de/media-neu/184/MAM_18491959/MAM_18491959_SHOP_IMAGE_2.0.png){: size="50%"}
<p class="caption">©️ Rossmann official website</p>

The tastiest of all the products I've tried so far, and convenient to carry around. Every now and then, the Rossmann app runs a promotion on organic products together with a discount on WellMix foods. That's when I sometimes buy them by the box with my invincible 10 % coupon. Around 70 cents each.

The flavors with 20 g of protein or more are white caramel, cookie dough, and vanilla. There are more flavors with less protein, but I haven't tried them. White caramel is my favorite. When I buy a lot at once, I mix it with cookie dough and alternate between them, but it's almost the only flavor I can eat every single day without getting tired of it.

Based on the white caramel flavor, 25 g of protein and 0.9 g of sugar per 50 g.
